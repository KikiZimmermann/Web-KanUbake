This page was created with a goal in mind

Just like most people, we've been running shopping streets and malls up and down when birthdays, weddings, invitations or special visits came up. In no less than the search for a worthy gift for a nice person. A friend, family, a colleague - but finding a fitting gift can be hard.

So we thought - what is something almost everyone likes? Something that can be personal, usable, a fitting gesture? We think we found the answer: A personalized cake!

However, if you don't know how to bake, are unable to or lack the time - and time it takes - you might make your way to a local confectionist. And then you want to make sure to have considered everything. The shape, the color, the decorations, the flavor, any allergies? Are you keeping track?

With our form, we help you to check and document every detail worth considering. So that you don't have to scribble notes and miss anything, and the cake becomes a complete success. It will help both you and the confectionist to clear everything up, so that they have an easier job and can ensure you truely get what you asked for. And you can sleep sound, because when you come to pick up the finished cake, it is what you expected.

Making your life easier.
Helping you save time.
Helping local business.
Everyone wins.

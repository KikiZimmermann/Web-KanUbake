How the Cake Designer works

The Cake Designer guides you through your cake request step by step. You will answer questions about the occasion, number of guests, cake shape, flavour, decoration and other important details.

The questionnaire adapts to your selections and only shows options that are relevant to your cake. It also helps identify combinations that may not work well together and takes allergies, dietary restrictions and other special requirements into account.

You do not need to know every detail yet. If you are unsure, you can choose “I’m not sure yet – please advise” and continue.

At the end, you will receive a summary of your choices, a suitable cake size recommendation and an estimated price range. The final result may vary depending on design complexity, ingredients, availability and special requests.

Welcome to the Cake Designer

This form helps you describe your cake idea step by step. You do not need to know every detail. If you are unsure, you can choose “I’m not sure yet – please advise”.

The cake size estimation is based on common cake serving charts and public cake size calculators. These sources show that the number of servings depends on cake shape, diameter, slice size and number of tiers. The pricing calculation is based on common custom cake pricing logic: ingredient costs, labor, overhead, complexity, delivery and profit margin. For the prototype, these factors are simplified into configurable pricing rules.
